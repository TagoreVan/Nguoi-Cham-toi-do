# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Young Vietnamese travellers who are choosing a local cultural trip and want a vivid, approachable introduction to the Cham heritage of Ninh Thuan.

## Product Purpose

Inspire visitors to plan a visit to Ninh Thuan through a one-page travel landing page centred on Cham towers and the Katê festival. Success means a visitor understands the destination, feels drawn to visit, and can clearly start planning a trip.

## Brand Personality

Youthful, celebratory, respectful.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic mass-tour templates, repeated feature-card grids, and decorative use of living Cham cultural symbols without context.

## Design Principles

- Let place, people, and heritage lead the story.
- Turn cultural interest into a clear next step.
- Use festival energy with care and clarity.
- Keep exploration accessible on small screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, semantic navigation, visible keyboard focus, useful image descriptions, and reduced-motion support.
